## Safety
Unchanged and tested: the learning engine imports nothing that decides, sizes, approves, executes or changes configuration; its only writes are attributions, insights, analytics snapshots, the calibration run log and its own job record (pinned by a source-scan test); recommendations are REVIEW or nothing; fixtures never calibrate; look-ahead is excluded; INSUFFICIENT_DATA is explicit; nothing is inferred (missing fields stay missing).

## Precision policy
Money is fixed-point (8 decimals) end to end. R-multiples and the exact ratio strings are truncated toward zero at 8 decimals (error < 1e-8). Win/loss rates are ratios of counts; the displayed `value` fields are ordinary numbers derived from the exact values.
