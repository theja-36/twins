Stage 09.0 · visual direction

TWINS Research Desk: mockups

Seven static mockups of the key screens, in dark (default) and light, at desktop, tablet and phone widths. They use synthetic, clearly labelled data and are not the application. Review the direction before any implementation begins.

1 · Desk (overview)TRADER home: what needs a decision, open paper positions, and how far each number can be trusted.Open →2 · Approval CenterThe safety-critical journey: a decision card with levels, stop validation, permitted size, and the approve / reject / cancel actions, plus every non-actionable state.Open →3 · Opportunity detail and researchOne candidate: levels, thirteen research agents, contradictions, lifecycle, and an explicit NOT AVAILABLE for comparable patterns.Open →4 · Paper trading and portfolioPositions, orders and fills with four distinct status vocabularies, portfolio facts, and the position detail with events.Open →5 · Learning and insightsEvidence with sample-size honesty: INSUFFICIENT DATA tiles, fixture provenance, snapshot freshness, REVIEW-only insights, and what is not available.Open →6 · ADMIN: platform overviewA different experience for administrators: services, safety posture, create-user, and the explicit boundary around traders' private data.Open →7 · States and componentsThe eight data states, status vocabularies, provenance and freshness markers, type, controls and colour tokens.Open →

What to look for

  • Honesty is visible. Every number shows whether it is fixture or live, current or stale, and whether the sample is large enough to say anything. Features that do not exist are shown as not available, never faked.
  • Four status vocabularies (decision, order, position, result) look different, so a filled order is never mistaken for an approved decision or a profit.
  • A calm desk, not a terminal. Serif titles, graphite surfaces, a single brass accent, hairlines instead of boxes, and tables and rows rather than a wall of cards.
  • Two experiences. Traders see the five workspaces; administrators see a console with their own navigation and an explicit boundary around private data.
  • Use the sun button to compare themes; add ?theme=light to any address to open it in light.
