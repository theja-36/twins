# Decision log (canonical for owner decisions)

**Register classes** (a recorded status is never evidence of implementation readiness):
- `DECIDED` - the owner decided (date). A DECIDED design direction is not implementation, deployment or commit approval.
- `DEFERRED` - the owner explicitly deferred it; the **affected work stays blocked** and is named. A deferral is never authorization to execute the blocked work.
- `UNRESOLVED` - no decision and no agreed deferral. Nothing UNRESOLVED may be assumed by an implementer.
- `PROPOSED` - suggested, not accepted.

No budget, key custody, vendor, reviewer, waiver, numerical tolerance or broker permission is invented anywhere in these documents. Information requests that are not decisions are in [OPEN_QUESTIONS.md](OPEN_QUESTIONS.md).
